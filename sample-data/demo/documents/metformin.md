# Metformin overview

Metformin is used to improve blood glucose control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus. It works primarily by decreasing hepatic glucose production and improving insulin sensitivity.

The briefing recommends beginning with a low dose and increasing the dose gradually when gastrointestinal tolerance permits. Common adverse effects include nausea, diarrhea, and abdominal discomfort.
